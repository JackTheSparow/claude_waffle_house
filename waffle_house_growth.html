The Spread of Waffle House

Waffle House numbers its restaurants roughly in the order they opened. Use that numbering as a clock and scroll — watch 2,005 locations fill in across the American South, lowest store number first.

The roots · ~1955 onward

It starts in Georgia and the Carolinas

The first Waffle House opened in 1955 in Avondale Estates, Georgia. The earliest surviving low-numbered stores still cluster tightly around metro Atlanta and the neighboring Carolinas — the chain's home turf.

Methods & caveats

Store number is a proxy for opening order — not an exact date. Waffle House assigns each restaurant a store number, and those numbers broadly increase in the order restaurants were opened. This page treats the numeric Store Code as a timeline and reveals locations from lowest to highest. That ordering is approximate, not precise: the dataset contains no opening dates, so "earlier number" means "opened earlier in sequence," not a specific year.

Three limitations follow from using numbers as a clock:

• Only currently-operating stores are shown. Restaurants that have since closed are absent, so some early/low numbers are sparse or missing — the early map is thinner than the chain actually was at the time.
• Numbering has gaps. Store codes are not a contiguous 1..N sequence (they run from 4 to 3442 with many gaps), so position in the sequence ≠ elapsed time.
• This is geography, not history. A dot's position is its current address; the animation shows where surviving stores sit, ordered by number, not a reconstructed historical timeline.

The 1955 Avondale Estates, Georgia origin is from Waffle House's published company history.
